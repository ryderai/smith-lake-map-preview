    Fishing on Smith Lake

    • What's in the lake. Alabama (spotted) bass, largemouth bass, striped bass, crappie, bream and catfish. (Alabama Living magazine — Smith Lake, Fish Alabama (Alabama Bass Trail) — Lewis Smith Lake)
    • The spotted bass record. Smith Lake once held the world record for spotted bass. That 8-pound, 15-ounce fish is still the Alabama state record. (Alabama Living magazine — Smith Lake)
    • Big stripers. Striped bass here reach the 40-pound range. Live shad is the preferred bait, and special striped bass rules apply. (Alabama Dept. of Conservation & Natural Resources — Smith Lake)
    • Where the fish hold. The water is deep, clear and blue. Most fishing is casting to the big rocks and fallen trees along the shoreline. (Alabama Dept. of Conservation & Natural Resources — Smith Lake)
    • Night fishing. Fish Alabama calls Smith Lake a hot spot for fly fishing at night. (Fish Alabama (Alabama Bass Trail) — Lewis Smith Lake)
    • Trout below the dam. The Sipsey Fork below Smith Dam is stocked with about 3,000 rainbow trout every month, on the third Thursday. Watch for generation: the water can rise 12 to 15 feet fast. Schedule: 1-800-LAKES-11. (Alabama Dept. of Conservation & Natural Resources — Sipsey Fork trout fishing below Smith Lake)

    • Sipsey Fork Trout Fishery (below Smith Dam)

      Alabama's year-round trout water. The state stocks about 3,000 rainbow trout (8 to 14 inches) every month, on the third Thursday. The first stretch below the dam is too deep to wade; bank and wade fishing runs from the Birmingham Water Works pump station down to the Highway 69 bridge. Limit: five fish, no size limit, no closed season. Water can rise 12 to 15 feet fast when the dam generates. Sirens warn you, and the schedule line is 1-800-LAKES-11.

    Smith Lake questions

    How big is Smith Lake?
    Smith Lake (Lewis Smith Lake) covers about 21,000 acres in Cullman, Walker and Winston counties in north Alabama, with more than 500 miles of shoreline. Sources differ on the exact numbers: the state wildlife agency says 21,200 acres, and Wikipedia lists 642 miles of shoreline.
    Where can I launch a boat on Smith Lake?
    Public launches on this map include Highway 63 Bridge Ramp, Smith Dam Ramp. Fee launches include Clear Creek Recreation Area Boat Ramp, Corinth Recreation Area, Houston Recreation Area, Smith Lake Park Boat Launch. The ramps we could confirm are on the map above. Tap one for directions, and call ahead about fees.
    Where can I fish from the bank on Smith Lake?
    Two piers to start with: the fishing pier at Smith Lake Park in Cullman (park entry fees apply) and the accessible Alabama Power pier at the Smith Dam tailrace. On the main lake, most fishing is casting to the big rocks and fallen trees along the shoreline, and Fish Alabama calls the lake a hot spot for fly fishing at night. Below the dam, the Sipsey Fork is stocked with rainbow trout every month.
    What fish are in Smith Lake?
    Alabama (spotted) bass, largemouth bass, striped bass, crappie, bream and catfish. Smith Lake once held the world record for spotted bass, an 8-pound, 15-ounce fish that is still the Alabama state record. Striped bass here reach the 40-pound range.
    Can you trout fish near Smith Lake?
    Yes. The Sipsey Fork below Lewis Smith Dam is stocked with about 3,000 rainbow trout every month, on the third Thursday. The limit is five fish, with no size limit and no closed season. The water can rise 12 to 15 feet quickly when the dam generates power. Sirens warn you, and the generation schedule line is 1-800-LAKES-11.
    How deep is Smith Lake?
    Wikipedia lists its deepest point at 264 feet. Other sources say close to 300 feet, so the exact figure depends on who measured it. The water is deep, clear and blue.
    How much does the water level drop in winter?
    Justin Dyar's Smith Lake guide gives a normal summer pool of 510 feet above sea level and a winter pool of 496 feet, so plan on a drop of about 14 feet each winter. Lot and dock depth matter when you buy on the lake.
    What towns are on Smith Lake?
    Lake towns include Arley, Bremen, Crane Hill, Double Springs, Houston and Logan, with Cullman and Jasper close by.
    Where can I rent a boat on Smith Lake?
    Boat and jet ski rental businesses on this map include Cappy's Boats, Evans Marine, Skier's Marine Smith Lake, Smith Lake Pontoon and Boat Rentals. Tap "Rent a boat" above the list to see them all, with directions and phone numbers.
    Are there waterfalls and hiking trails near Smith Lake?
    Yes. Bankhead National Forest sits on the north and west side of the lake. This map shows 7 waterfalls, including Caney Creek Falls, Holmes Chapel Falls, Kinlock Falls, plus trailheads and Forest Service recreation areas. Some waterfall trails are unmarked, so check the Forest Service page before you go.
